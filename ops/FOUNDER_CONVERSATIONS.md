# Founder conversations (Week 1 and 2)

**Goal:** before the engine writes its first email, hear from 10–15 founders of UP companies incorporated in the last six months. We need to know whether the value-first approach (your deadlines, your schemes, claim your page) is something they would welcome, and how they want to be reached.

## Who and how

- **Who:** founders of UP private limited companies or LLPs incorporated since May 2026, mixed across sectors and districts (not only Noida and Lucknow). At least three should not be tech companies.
- **How to reach them:** warm routes only. Incubators we know, IIT and E-Cell networks, Startup in UP events, our own platform users, and people the team already knows. **Not** cold messages to numbers or emails taken from the data. The engine's rules apply to us too.
- **Format:** a 15–20 minute call or meeting, in Hindi or English, as the founder prefers. Ask before taking notes, and record nothing without permission.

## Opening (read or adapt)

> We are building a service that tells new companies in UP which legal deadlines are coming up and which government schemes they may qualify for. Before we build it, we would like to learn from founders like you. There is nothing to buy. Can I ask you a few questions for about 15 minutes?

## Questions

1. When was the company incorporated, and what does it do?
2. In the first months, which compliance tasks surprised you or came late? (board meeting, auditor, INC-20A, share certificates, GST, others)
3. Who handles compliance for you today: a CA, CS, yourself, or nobody yet? What do you pay, roughly?
4. Did you look for government schemes or grants? Which ones, and how did you find them?
5. Since incorporating, have you received unsolicited calls, emails or WhatsApp messages from service providers? How many, and how did they make you feel?
6. If an email showed you your company's next three deadlines and two schemes you may qualify for, with the reason for each, would you open the link? What would make you trust it, or distrust it?
7. How would you prefer to receive deadline reminders: email, WhatsApp, a call, or not at all? In Hindi or English?
8. What would make you pay for help with any of this? What would be too much?
9. Who else should we talk to? (Ask for an introduction, never for a contact list.)

## Afterwards

- Write the notes in `founder_conversations.csv` the same day (a copy of `founder_conversations.example.csv`; the filled-in file is kept out of git). Use the company name and your summary; no phone numbers or personal emails.
- After every 5 conversations, note what changed our mind. The first email draft (Phase 3) will be tested against these notes.
