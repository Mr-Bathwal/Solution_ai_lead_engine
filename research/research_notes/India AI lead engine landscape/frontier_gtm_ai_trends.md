## 1. Which AI GTM / sales-agent / data startups launched or raised in 2025–2026, and what novel approach does each take?

### Takeaway
Money is going to (a) **AI-native "systems of action"** that replace the CRM and the point tools around it (Monaco, Rox, Actively, Unify), (b) **agentic web-research and data infrastructure** (Parallel, Exa, Floqer, Browser Use), (c) **learning from outcomes** (Hightouch AI Decisioning, Encore), (d) **simulation and AI-search visibility** (Simile, Aaru, Profound), and (e) in India, **vernacular voice agents and call screening** (Ringg, Sarvam Samvaad, Equal AI). YC's 2026 batches moved from "AI SDR" toward per-visitor sales agents, agent-commerce plumbing and the "agent trust" layer. Total sales, marketing and CRM startup funding in 2026 passed about $3.7B (Crunchbase, via secondary sources).

## 2. What are the new technical ideas (deep research agents, browser agents, signals, knowledge graphs, restraint, RL/bandits, synthetic buyers, inbound+outbound unification, agent-to-agent, MCP, warehouse-native)?

### Takeaway
The technical frontier has moved from writing a message to deciding: what is true about this account right now (a continuously refreshed, agent-researched graph), whether to contact it at all, through which channel, and what was learned from the result. Two new external forces matter. Buyers now use AI agents (about 40% of US B2B buyers per Deloitte), and the agent protocols (MCP, A2A, AP2) are maturing. Benchmarks show general LLM agents are still unreliable at multi-step CRM work, which favours narrow, verifiable pipelines over fully open-ended autonomy.

### Cited Findings

**Agentic web research and continuous monitoring**
- Parallel offers Search, Task, Extract and **Monitor** APIs for long-running background agents. — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/28/parag-agrawals-startup-raises-100m-build-parallel-web-ai-agents/)
- Floqer agents build lists from a natural-language description of the target, then keep records fresh by watching for job changes, promotions and funding events. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/14/floqers-ai-agents-help-b2b-revenue-teams-research-customers/)
- Browser-automation agents are now funded as their own category (Browser Use, $17M seed). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2984735)

**Signals and the "Know layer"**
- Common Room argues the first step is an identity-resolved, person-level "Know" layer that combines signals (job changes, pricing-page visits, product usage, community activity). The system should **decide whether to engage before deciding what to say**. AI should do the mechanical work (list hygiene, research drafts, routing, follow-up on clear signals) and humans should keep judgment calls (tone, multi-threading, escalation). — [Common Room, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.commonroom.io/blog/ai-sdr/) (vendor)
- LeanData's 2026 survey of 157 revenue leaders (via Common Room): **94% say their GTM infrastructure isn't AI-ready**, 55% cite data quality as the top AI challenge, and 70% say poor data undermines execution. — [Common Room](https://www.commonroom.io/blog/ai-sdr/)
- Unify's signal-triggered "Plays" (40+ data sources) report a 9.1% positive reply rate on product-usage signals. — [Unify](https://www.unifygtm.com/blog/series-b)
- Typical signal taxonomy: first-party web, review-site intent, third-party topic surge, event signals (funding, hiring, tech stack) and champion moves. Bombora-class intent data costs about $25–30k a year. — [Artisan signal guide](https://www.artisan.co/blog/signal-based-selling-the-complete-guide-to-intent-signals) (vendor)

**Learning loops (RL, bandits, interaction mining)**
- Hightouch AI Decisioning uses RL agents to choose content, offer, channel and timing per user, natively on the customer's data warehouse. — [Hightouch](https://hightouch.com/blog/ai-in-marketing-automation)
- Encore mines historical conversations by stage to learn which moves advance deals, then trains voice agents on them. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/29/encore-ai-raises-30m-to-build-ai-agents-that-learn-from-customer-calls/)

**Synthetic buyer simulation**
- Simile ($2B valuation, Jul 2026) and Aaru (Dec 2025) sell generative-agent populations for marketing and product research. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/synthetic-user-startup-simile-raises-200m-at-2b-valuation-5-months-after-100m-series-a/); [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/sources-ai-synthetic-research-startup-233859223.html)
- Critique: no accuracy metrics have been published, and simulations reflect what people say online, not what they do. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/synthetic-user-startup-simile-raises-200m-at-2b-valuation-5-months-after-100m-series-a/)

**Agent-to-agent sales and buyer-side agents**
- Deloitte (survey of 1,000+ US suppliers and buyers; research Jan 2026, article 26 Jun 2026): **about 40% of B2B buyers already use agentic AI in purchasing** (evaluation, configuration, contract review, price benchmarking). **24% of suppliers use agents in sales and 67% plan to.** Its advice is to make data "machine-readable, timely, enriched, and verifiable" and to build APIs. 92% of buyers who use EDI plan to move partly or fully to other channels. — [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/what-we-do/capabilities/applied-artificial-intelligence/articles/b2b-agentic-commerce.html)
- Forrester projection (secondary): about **1 in 5 B2B sellers will face agent-led quote negotiations by end-2026**. — [eLogic](https://elogic.co/blog/ai-agents-b2b-buying/)
- Vendor projection: 90% of B2B buying will be AI-agent-intermediated by 2028 (about $15T). This is aggressive and secondary. — [commercetools](https://commercetools.com/blog/ai-trends-shaping-agentic-commerce)
- Staged path: content becomes machine-readable, then answerable, then negotiation, then full agent-to-agent transactions around 2028–2030. — [Salespeak](https://salespeak.ai/blog/agent-to-agent-commerce/) (vendor)
- Protocol gap: there is no standard way to express customer-specific contract pricing, entitlements or approval authority, and no machine-verifiable credential that an agent can commit a company to a purchase. — [Blue Bolt Solutions](https://www.blueboltsolutions.com/agentic-commerce-b2b-manufacturing-2026/)
- **Google A2A (9 Apr 2025)**: an open agent-to-agent protocol. Agents advertise their abilities in JSON "Agent Cards" and work through long-running tasks with a defined lifecycle. Google positions it as a complement to MCP. It launched with 50+ partners, including Salesforce, SAP, ServiceNow, Infosys, TCS and Wipro. — [Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/a2a-a-new-era-of-agent-interoperability/)
- **Google AP2 (17 Sep 2025)**: Agent Payments Protocol. Cryptographically signed **Intent Mandates** and **Cart Mandates** backed by verifiable credentials create a non-repudiable audit trail. It extends A2A and MCP, and 60+ organizations back it (Mastercard, AmEx, PayPal, Salesforce and others). — [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-agents-to-payments-ap2-protocol)
- YC S26 Rindler exposes websites to agents as structured data, and Palisade gives each site visitor its own sales agent. — [Extruct S26](https://www.extruct.ai/data-room/ycombinator-companies-s26/)

**MCP-based GTM tooling**
- HubSpot's remote MCP server (mcp.hubspot.com) has been reported GA since Apr 2026. Apollo's MCP server works in Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor (search, enrich, sequences, CRM updates). Clay's MCP is in open beta with per-rep credit budgets. Salesforce, Attio, ZoomInfo (read-only), Outreach, Salesloft and Explorium also have MCP servers. — [pipe0](https://pipe0.com/blog/best-mcp-servers-gtm); [Onfire](https://www.onfire.ai/blog/best-mcp-tools-for-sales-teams) (vendor roundups)

**AI-search visibility (GEO) as the new inbound**
- Profound tracks how AI models describe and recommend brands across millions of real prompts. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/02/24/exclusive-as-ai-threatens-search-profound-raises-96-million-to-help-brands-stay-visible/)
- An academic survey covers GEO research from 2023 to 2026. — [arXiv 2607.14035](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.14035)

**Agent reliability evidence**
- Salesforce AI Research's **CRMArena-Pro** (arXiv 2505.18878, May 2025; 4,280 tasks, 19 business activities): top LLM agents reach about **58% single-turn success and about 35% multi-turn**. Workflow execution exceeds 83%. Agents show **"near-zero" inherent confidentiality awareness**, and nearly half of Gemini 2.5 Pro's multi-turn failures came from not asking for crucial information. — [alphaXiv overview](https://alphaxiv.org/overview/2505.18878v1); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/salesforces-crm-benchmark-finds-ai-agents-struggle-in-real-world-business-scenarios/)

**Agent trust layer**
- YC P26 includes companies that insure agent behaviour (Klaimee, Mount) and pre-check agent actions (Clawvisor). — [Extruct](https://www.extruct.ai/research/ycx26/)

### Inferences
- **Warehouse-native and MCP-native** architecture is now the default expectation. A lead engine should treat its account graph as a queryable store and expose it as an MCP server, so founders can work their pipeline from inside Claude or ChatGPT.
- "Restraint" exists today as a vendor philosophy (Common Room, Unify signals). I found no product that markets an explicit **contact budget / do-not-send policy** as its core feature. That is open whitespace.
- Given CRMArena-Pro's 35% multi-turn success, autonomous multi-step actions such as negotiation or CRM writes should be gated with verification steps. Narrow, high-reliability workflows, where agents score above 83%, should be autonomous first.
- AP2-style signed mandates are a ready-made pattern for **verifiable consent and authority**: an "outreach mandate" signed by the buyer, or a record that the seller's agent is authorized to make an offer.

### Gaps
- I found no published arXiv paper or product benchmark showing **bandit or RL optimization of B2B cold outreach** with reply-rate or meeting-rate outcomes. Hightouch's RL claims are vendor-only.
- I found no independent accuracy validation of synthetic buyer panels for B2B message testing.
- OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol / Instant Checkout page returned 403 and could not be verified in this session.
- Knowledge-graph GTM (beyond Rox's "agent swarm" framing) found no dedicated 2026 startup in this session.

## 3. What are thought leaders and practitioners saying (AI SDR flood critique; signal-based, warm, permission-based, product-led)?

### Takeaway
Practitioners agree on the diagnosis: **volume-first AI SDRs damaged deliverability and trust, and many deployments were cancelled.** Two prescriptions dominate. One is **signal-based / warm / "why now" outreach with humans in judgment roles.** The other is treating agents as **supervised staff that need daily training, not set-and-forget software.** On the recipient side (HN), people want disclosure, a cited source, a single message and visible reasoning. VCs are shifting the debate to **how agent outcomes are sold and priced** (outcome-based models).

### Cited Findings

**Adoption versus results**
- G2 (2026 "AI in B2B Marketing" report, via LinkedOtter): **44% of B2B sales teams have deployed AI SDRs**. Cold-email reply rates fell to **3.43% in 2026 from 5.1%** the year before (Warmly data). — [LinkedOtter](https://www.linkedotter.com/articles/ai-sdr-44-percent-b2b-sales-adoption-june-2026) (secondary)
- A cold-email operator claims "around 80% of the sales teams I speak to cancelled their AI SDR inside 3 months". This is anecdotal. — [@coldemailchris on X](https://x.com/coldemailchris/status/2113057774951407905)

**Critiques and prescriptions**
- **Common Room critique** (22 Sep 2026): "AI SDR" is a vague label; volume-first tools damage sender reputation; agents acting on stale data "make mistakes faster". It cites Gartner (Aug 2025) that **by 2030, 75% of B2B buyers will prefer sales experiences that prioritize human interaction over AI**. — [Common Room](https://www.commonroom.io/blog/ai-sdr/)
- **The 11x cautionary tale** (TechCrunch, 24 Mar 2025): it displayed customer logos (ZoomInfo, Airtable) from trials that were never converted. Employees alleged ARR was counted from contracts with 3-month break clauses (one example: about $14M reported versus about $3M of contracts that passed the trial) and described churn of 70–80%. 11x said it uses contracted ARR and that current retention is 79%. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have/)
- **SaaStr / Jason Lemkin** (Lenny's Podcast and SaaStr posts, 2025–26): SaaStr went from about 10 salespeople to about 20 AI agents with 1.2 humans and kept the same revenue. Later posts say 2.5–3 humans run an eight-figure business on a fleet of agents. Lemkin stresses agents are not set-and-forget, and SaaStr adopted outbound agents before inbound because a mediocre agent risks less on cold prospects than on hot inbound leads. All figures are self-reported. — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/jason-lenny-are-back-the-real-future-of-ai-in-sales/); [SaaStr: the 20 agents](https://www.saastr.com/stop-learning-ai-start-doing-ai-the-20-agents-running-saastr); [Lenny's Podcast episode](https://pod.wave.co/podcast/lennys-podcast-product-career-growth-b65486a6-7cff-4966-bbea-2bc239e90aa4/we-replaced-our-sales-team-with-20-ai-agentsheres-what-happened-jason-lemkin-saastr); [Development Corporate analysis](https://developmentcorporate.com/startups/what-really-happened-when-saastr-replaced-its-sales-team-with-ai-agents/)

**VC frames**
- **a16z** on selling agents that own outcomes, in three models: license the platform (new budget, slower), take over work that is already outsourced (existing budget, faster), or go full-stack and become the business. — [a16z on X](https://x.com/a16z/status/2110430504860401675)
- a16z expects the sales motion itself to keep changing as reps hand off data entry and account research. — [a16z](https://a16z.com/need-for-speed-in-ai-sales-ai-doesnt-just-change-what-you-sell-it-also-changes-how-you-sell-it/)

- A secondary summary of 2026 VC predictions mentions buyers posting **bounties for outcomes** (for example, paying per meeting) with agents competing for them. — [The AI Opportunities](https://www.theaiopportunities.com/p/the-full-2026-vc-ai-predictions-where) (not verified against a16z's original)

**Hacker News (recipient voice)**
- "Ask HN: How do you handle cold outreach emails?": AI-scraped lead funnels made cold outreach "orders of magnitude worse", and AI follow-ups turn passive-aggressive. — [HN 46733696](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46733696)

- A commenter would only use a tool that **shows why each personalized element is relevant and lets them review every email**. — [HN 46733696](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46733696)
- **DB Pro (31 Aug 2026)**: after an HN comment, the author got two **self-disclosed AI-agent cold emails** (Razzlo/iLands and Organ's "CMO agent"). ### Inferences
- The credible 2026 positioning is a **trust-first, signal-first** engine: few, well-justified touches with visible reasoning and agent disclosure. This matches what recipients ask for and what vendors with the best numbers (Unify, Common Room) promote.

- SaaStr's "outbound first, inbound later" finding suggests agents should earn autonomy level by level.

## 4. How are buyers' inboxes and phones responding (Gmail AI, Apple, Truecaller, Google Call Screen), and what does that imply for outbound in 2026–2027?

### Takeaway
Both main channels now have **AI gatekeepers**. Gmail layers relevance ranking and Gemini summaries on top of strict bulk-sender rules. iOS 26, Pixel, Truecaller and India's Equal AI answer unknown calls and ask the caller for their name and reason. Outbound in 2026–27 has to pass a machine judge first: authenticated identity, a clear reason stated in the first line or first seconds, and low complaint rates. The judge is increasingly the recipient's own agent.

- **Design for the AI screener.** The voice agent's first sentence must state who is calling, on what verified business identity, and why, in a form a screening AI will pass on. Equal AI already plans outbound calls on users' behalf.
- **Email copy should be summary-proof.** Lead with the specific "why you, why now" observation in the first line, assuming Gemini or Apple will summarize it. Complaint rate, not open rate, is the metric that keeps a sender alive.
- Channel choice should be decided per lead, based on reachability and relationship, rather than run as a fixed email-then-call sequence.

## 5. India-specific frontier players and data-sharing rails (Indian AI GTM startups, Sarvam-powered agents, AA/ONDC-style consented data)

### Takeaway
India's frontier is in **vernacular voice and WhatsApp agents** (Sarvam Samvaad, Ringg, Bolna, Gnani) and **AI call screening** (Equal AI, Truecaller). I found no India-native, venture-scale **AI lead engine for discovering Indian SMBs**, which is whitespace. **Regulation: TRAI / TCCCPR**
- **Feb 2025 amendment**:
  - commercial calls must use designated number series, and telemarketers may not use ordinary 10-digit numbers
  - inferred consent lasts only as long as the contractual relationship
  - explicit consent for a transaction is valid for 7 days
  - the enforcement trigger is now **5 complaints in 10 days**, and action against unregistered senders must come within 5 days
- **Aug 2024 directions**: disconnection plus blacklisting of up to 2 years for violating promotional calls. 140-series calls migrated to DLT on 1 Oct 2024, and the 160 series was created for service and transactional calls.
- **Consent infrastructure**: Digital Consent Acquisition (2023), and an RBI Digital Consent Management pilot (Jun 2025).
- **Litigation**: IndiaMart v. TRAI was heard in the Delhi HC in Mar 2026.
- **AI-specific rules**: TRAI is reportedly considering AI-specific telemarketing, disclosure and consent-verification amendments, none in force as of 16 Mar 2026.

They say a Sep 2026 "TCCCPR Third Amendment" keeps the two separate, and that AI or autodialled calls must be pre-declared as A2P or are treated as spam. — [Caller.digital: DPDP vs TRAI](https://www.caller.digital/blog/dpdp-vs-trai-consent-voice-recordings-audit-trail-india-2026); [The Telephony](https://www.thetelephony.co/india/blog/dpdp-outbound-ai-calls-guide) (vendor; verify against the Gazette)

**Regulation: DPDP**
- The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 Nov 2025. **Rule 4 (Consent Manager registration) is scheduled for about 13 Nov 2026**, and notice, consent and penalty duties for outbound and AI calls are cited from about 13 May 2027. Penalties reach up to ₹250 crore. — [Qualia Bits](https://qualiabits.com/blog/dpdp-call-qa-compliance-india/); [The Telephony](https://www.thetelephony.co/india/blog/dpdp-outbound-ai-calls-guide) (vendor/secondary; dates should be confirmed with MeitY)

**Consented data rails**
- **Account Aggregator** (Sahamati, 2 Sep 2026): 500M+ fulfilled consents; 1,100+ regulated entities live; 2.8B+ accounts enabled; FY26 AA-enabled lending of ₹3.82 lakh crore across 3.68 crore loans, which is **8.4% of retail and MSME lending by value**. ### Inferences

## 6. Synthesis: 8–12 frontier capabilities that would make a lead engine category-defining in 2026–2027

### Takeaway
A category-defining 2026–27 lead engine should be built as a **continuously learning, consent-native decision system that sells to humans and their agents**, not as a faster sequencer. Below are 12 capabilities, each with evidence and current examples. The India-specific twist is vernacular voice and WhatsApp, fresh-incorporation timing, and the TRAI/DPDP consent stack.

### Cited Findings

**1. A living account graph maintained by research-agent fleets**, rather than a static database. Agents re-verify facts on events such as incorporation, GST registration, hiring, funding or a website going live.

**2. A restraint engine (whether, not just what).** An explicit "should we contact this lead at all, and when" decision, with a send budget per account and per domain, a default of not sending, and visible reasoning.

### Inferences
- **Most differentiated for SolutionAI**: capabilities 2 (restraint), 9 (screening-aware vernacular voice), 10 (consent-native) and 1 applied to *newly incorporated Indian companies*. US players are weak on all four in India: Indic languages, TRAI/DPDP, MCA/GST data. The voice and dialer layer alone is commoditizing (Ringg's "not sticky" point), so it should be rented (Sarvam- or Ringg-class) rather than built.
- **The timing signal is intrinsic.** A newly registered company has predictable needs in its first 30–180 days: a bank account, GST, accounting, payroll, a website, insurance and compliance filings. Combining this with restraint means the engine can say "the right three vendors, at the right week, in Hindi, on WhatsApp", which is closer to a curated, permissioned introduction than to cold spam. This is an inference from the evidence above, not a sourced finding.
- **Plan for buyer-agent reality.** By 2027 a meaningful share of the "prospects" the engine reaches may be screening agents (Equal AI, Truecaller, Gmail Gemini). Agent-legible identity and an agent-to-agent negotiation protocol (A2A-style) are early bets that match where the funding is going.
- **Metrics design**: optimize for positive replies, meetings held and consented opt-ins. Keep complaint rate as a hard constraint. Do not report opens, since AI auto-opening inflates them.

### Gaps
- None of the 12 capabilities has independent, peer-reviewed evidence of ROI in B2B outbound. Most proof is vendor-reported or early-stage.
- I found no live example of a production **seller-agent to buyer-agent B2B negotiation**. Deloitte and Forrester describe early, assisted stages.
- I found no India-specific study of AI-call screening adoption among MSME owners, or of WhatsApp Business API cost and limits for AI-driven outreach in 2026. Neither was researched because the search budget ran out.
